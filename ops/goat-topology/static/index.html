What these words mean
router
The process that carries messages between applications. One or more per site.
routing group
A set of routers that share a map of each other. Called a region in the configuration. Splitting routers into groups is what keeps the maps small.
north / south
Two halves of a router's world. North is the group it belongs to as an equal. South is everything hanging below it, which it terminates rather than routes through.
route entry
One line in a router's table saying "to reach X, send to Y". A group of N routers costs every member N×(N−1) of them.
ZID
A name a zenoh process announces for itself. Useful for telling processes apart. Not a credential — nothing verifies it.
not reporting
A router another router's map lists, that sends no report of its own: a partner's router, or one of ours with no publisher on its host. Its links are real; its settings and load are unknown.
stale
This router has not reported recently. Its numbers are the last ones we saw, not current ones.
