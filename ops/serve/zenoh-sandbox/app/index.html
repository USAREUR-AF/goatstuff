Watch messages move across the fabric.

Zenoh moves data between things that don't know about each other. Someone publishes to a topic; whoever subscribes to a matching topic hears it; and a queryable can answer questions on demand. Try it below — then open this page in a second tab and watch the two talk.

🛟 This is a throwaway sandbox. The router it talks to peers with nothing and stores nothing — there's no login and nothing to break. Experiment freely.

brokerless — a publisher's data goes straight to the subscribers that match

Publish

i To publish is to put a value onto a topic. You don't address anyone — you just announce it. Whoever is subscribed to a matching topic hears it; if nobody is, it simply isn't heard. No queues, no broker mailbox. put a value onto the fabric

Query & Reply

i Publish/subscribe (above) pushes a stream — a subscriber hears every reading as it happens. Query/reply pulls: a queryable stands ready to answer a topic, and a get sends a query that reaches every matching queryable and collects their replies. Same fabric, opposite direction. get · query · queryable · reply

🌡️ The sensor above streams the temperature (subscribe to hear every reading). Here you do the other half: stand up a queryable that answers demo/temperature, then ask it — "what's the temperature right now?" — and get a reply back.
