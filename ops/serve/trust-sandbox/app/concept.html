Admission is established once at onboarding — independent of the identity below. Being on the mesh is not trust. A signed identity you present per connection — a scoped, time-limited, revocable keycard. SPIFFE is a standard for workload identity; SPIRE implements it. Identity is earned by attestation and re-proven continuously — a leaked credential is useless within minutes. Ory authenticates the human + role — it does not issue. A role authorizes admin actions, like a CA mint for a machine. SPIRE issues workload identities automatically by attestation — no human in the loop. The per-write decision, keyed to the identity above. A denied write is silent — the sender is told nothing.

How it composes with the rest

These five aspects are complementary and coordinated, not a sequence you pass through. A real action uses only the ones that apply — a machine write uses NMD, a workload NWD, a human signing in to act NH.

Why this isn't enough on its own

the gap the other aspects fill

That gap is the whole reason there are five complementary aspects rather than one. Keep only this one and a specific protection disappears — which is exactly what the playground lets you prove by changing a single thing and watching what breaks.
